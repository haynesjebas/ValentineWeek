Before the ton and all its knowing eyes,
may I have the honour of being your Valentine?

What is most precious is often kept from the world, and so I give you these words quietly.

Your "Yes" was not overheard by the ton, nor announced to any curious ear yet it has settled most firmly with me, as though something long unsettled has finally found its place.

In that single word, you offered me a confidence I did not know I lacked, and a warmth I find myself returning to without effort.

Let this Valentine remain ours alone, known only in glances and in moments stolen from the ordinary. I find I prefer it that way; choosing you, quietly, again and again.

❦
